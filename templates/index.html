How FairSight Works

A transparent, multi-metric approach to fairness auditing

📏

Disparate Impact (80% Rule)

Measures whether the selection rate for a disadvantaged group is at least 80% of the most advantaged group. The legal standard used by the EEOC.

DI = min(rate) / max(rate)
⚖️

Statistical Parity Difference

Measures the raw gap in positive outcome rates between groups. A difference of 0 means all groups receive the same proportion of positive outcomes.

SPD = P(Ŷ=1|A=0) − P(Ŷ=1|A=1)
🎯

Equalized Odds

Requires equal true positive rates AND false positive rates across groups. Prevents both under-serving and over-penalizing minority groups.

TPR_A = TPR_B, FPR_A = FPR_B
🔀

Intersectional Analysis

Examines bias across combinations of sensitive attributes (e.g., gender × race). Single-attribute analysis often misses compounding discrimination.

P(Y|A=a, B=b) vs P(Y|A=a', B=b')

Why This Matters

Algorithmic systems now make consequential decisions about who gets a job, a loan, medical treatment, or parole. When these systems learn from historically biased data, they don't just reflect past discrimination — they amplify and automate it at scale.

FairSight gives organizations a transparent, accessible tool to audit their systems before deployment — not after harm is done.
